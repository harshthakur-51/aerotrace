The release includes measured observations, saved diagnoses, comparison outputs, sealed evaluation inputs and separate experiment records.

| Evidence | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| [Five-fault sequence study](../docs/FAULT-SEQUENCE-STUDY.md) | All 1,081 onset histories exercised in software, with nine archived airborne recordings and explicit timing assumptions |
| [Engine 2 drive contrasts](engine-drive-20261106-01/REPORT.md) | Loss of shared engine drive versus two local source failures, with Yellow hydraulic backup varied |
| [Timed generator and Blue pump cases](../docs/TEMPORAL-STUDY.md) | Near-synchronous, staggered and recovery-gap responses |
| [Paired development flight](pressure-pair-20261106-01/RESULT.md) | Generator output and Blue pressure loss with retained bus voltage and indicated quantity |
| [Sealed version 2 evaluation](pressure-v2-prospective-20261106-01/REPORT.md) | Six planned episodes, two simulator flight groups and final live/replay agreement |
| [Sealed version 1 pilot](prospective-pilot-20261103-01/PILOT-REPORT.md) | Four planned cases covering reference, generator, inventory and paired effects |
| [Quantity indication stress](quantity-indication-stress-20261106-01/README.md) | Results with early and late false quantity declines |
| [Update cost and replay parity](pressure-v2-load-optimized-20261106-01/REPORT.md) | Local processing timings and 276 checked native prefixes |

The published files retain their recorded bytes. Historical collection manifests may also name local simulator startup scripts, panel templates and captured operator source kept in the development archive. Those files are outside the public evidence selection. The sealed prediction and scoring sources are included for the [reproduction workflow](../docs/REPRODUCING.md).
